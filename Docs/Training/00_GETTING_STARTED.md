# 🚀 Getting Started

> **For:** All Users | **Time to Read:** 5 minutes

This guide covers the basics that every user needs to know: logging in, managing your account, and navigating the system.

---

## 📍 Accessing the System

The Chesanto Bakery Management System is accessed through a web browser.

**URL:** `https://chesanto.railway.app` (or the URL provided by your administrator)

**Supported Browsers:**
- Google Chrome (recommended)
- Mozilla Firefox
- Safari
- Microsoft Edge

---

## 🔐 Logging In

### First Time Login

1. Open your web browser
2. Go to the system URL
3. Click **"Login"**
4. Enter your credentials:
   - **Email:** Your work email (e.g., `yourname@chesanto.co.ke`)
   - **Password:** The temporary password provided to you
5. Click **"Sign In"**

### Changing Your Password (First Login)

On your first login, you will be required to change your password:

1. Enter your **current password** (the temporary one)
2. Enter your **new password** (must be at least 8 characters)
3. **Confirm** your new password
4. Click **"Change Password"**

**Password Requirements:**
- Minimum 8 characters
- Mix of letters and numbers recommended
- Do not share your password with anyone

### Regular Login

After setting up your password:

1. Go to the system URL
2. Enter your **email** and **password**
3. Click **"Sign In"**
4. You will be taken to your dashboard

---

## 🏠 The Dashboard

After logging in, you'll see your dashboard. What you see depends on your role:

| Role | Dashboard Shows |
|------|-----------------|
| Superadmin | Full system overview, all modules |
| Admin | Financial summaries, reports access |
| Production Manager | Production stats, stock levels |
| Dispatch | Today's dispatches, stock available |
| Salesman | Your dispatches, your commissions |
| Security | Gate log access |

### Navigation Menu

The **sidebar menu** (left side) shows the modules you can access:

- Click on a menu item to go to that section
- Your current location is highlighted
- Menu items you cannot access are hidden (not shown)

---

## 👤 Managing Your Profile

### Viewing Your Profile

1. Click on your **name** in the top-right corner
2. Select **"Profile"** from the dropdown
3. You'll see your profile information

### Updating Your Profile

You can update the following information:

1. Go to your **Profile**
2. Click **"Edit Profile"**
3. Update your details:
   - First Name
   - Last Name
   - Phone Number (primary)
   - Phone Number (secondary) - optional
   - Profile Photo - optional
4. Click **"Save Changes"**

**Note:** You cannot change your own email address or role. Contact an administrator if these need to be updated.

---

## 🔑 Password Management

### Changing Your Password

1. Go to your **Profile**
2. Click **"Change Password"**
3. Enter your **current password**
4. Enter your **new password**
5. Confirm your new password
6. Click **"Update Password"**

### Forgot Your Password?

If you forget your password:

1. Go to the login page
2. Click **"Forgot Password?"**
3. Enter your **email address**
4. Click **"Send Reset Link"**
5. Check your email for a password reset link
6. Click the link and set a new password

**Note:** The reset link expires after 24 hours. If it expires, request a new one.

---

## 🚪 Logging Out

Always log out when you're done, especially on shared computers:

1. Click on your **name** in the top-right corner
2. Select **"Logout"**
3. You will be returned to the login page

**Security Tip:** The system will automatically log you out after a period of inactivity.

---

## ⚠️ Common Issues

### "Access Denied" Message

If you see an "Access Denied" message:
- You're trying to access a page your role doesn't have permission for
- Contact your supervisor if you believe you need access

### "Invalid Credentials" on Login

If your login fails:
- Check that your email is correct
- Check that Caps Lock is not on
- Try resetting your password
- Contact an administrator if the problem persists

### Page Not Loading

If pages are slow or not loading:
- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the page (F5 or Ctrl+R)
- Try clearing your browser cache
- Try a different browser

---

## 📱 Mobile Access

The system works on mobile devices (phones and tablets), but is optimized for desktop/laptop use. For the best experience:

- Use a tablet or computer for data entry
- Mobile is fine for viewing dispatches and quick lookups
