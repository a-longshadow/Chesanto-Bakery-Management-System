# 💼 Salesman Guide

> **Role:** Sales Representative | **Access Level:** Own Dispatches & Commissions Only

As a Salesman, you view your assigned dispatches and track your commission earnings. This guide covers everything you need to know.

---

## 🎯 What You Can Do

- View dispatches assigned to you
- See your sales history
- Track your commission earnings
- View your profile

**Note:** You cannot create dispatches or process returns. The Dispatch Officer handles that.

---

## 🏠 Your Dashboard

When you log in, you'll see your personal dashboard showing:

- Your recent dispatches
- Today's dispatch (if any)
- Your commission summary

---

## 📋 My Dispatches

**Location:** Sales → **My Dispatches**

### Viewing Your Dispatches

1. Go to **Sales** → **My Dispatches**
2. See all dispatches assigned to you

### Dispatch Information

Each dispatch shows:

| Column | What It Means |
|--------|---------------|
| Dispatch # | The unique dispatch number |
| Date | When you took the products |
| Products | What products and quantities you took |
| Crates | How many crates you took |
| Status | Dispatched or Returned |
| Revenue | Your sales total (after return) |
| Commission | Your commission earned |

### Dispatch Status

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Dispatched** | You're currently out with these products |
| **Returned** | You've returned and the dispatch is complete |

### Filtering Your Dispatches

- **Date range:** View today, this week, this month
- **Status:** See only pending or only completed

### Viewing Dispatch Details

Click on a dispatch number to see full details:
- All products dispatched
- Quantities dispatched
- What you returned
- What you sold
- Revenue breakdown
- Commission breakdown

## 💰 My Commissions

**Location:** Sales → **My Commissions**

### Understanding Your Commission

Your commission is calculated as:

```
Commission = Sales Revenue × Commission Rate
```

For example:
- You sold KES 35,000 worth of products
- Your commission rate is 7%
- Your commission = 35,000 × 0.07 = **KES 2,450**

### Viewing Your Commissions

1. Go to **Sales** → **My Commissions**
2. See your commission summary:
   - Total commission this month
   - Commission per dispatch
   - Commission rate

### Commission Details

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Date | Dispatch date |
| Dispatch # | Which dispatch |
| Revenue | Total sales from that dispatch |
| Commission | Amount you earned |

### Commission Calculation

Commission is calculated when your dispatch is processed (when you return):

1. Dispatch Officer processes your return
2. System calculates: Sold = Dispatched - Returned
3. Revenue = Sold × Product Prices
4. Commission = Revenue × Your Rate

---

## 📊 Understanding Your Numbers

### What Counts as "Sold"

```
Sold = Dispatched - Returned
```

Example:
- You took 50 Bread
- You returned 5 Bread
- You sold 45 Bread

### Revenue Calculation

Each product has a selling price:
- Bread: KES 60
- Scones: KES 50
- KDF: KES 100
- Family Bread 800g: KES 80

Your revenue = Sum of (Quantity Sold × Price) for each product

### Sales Target

You may have a monthly sales target. Check with your supervisor about:
- What your target is
- How close you are to reaching it
- Any bonuses for exceeding target

## 📱 Daily Workflow

### Morning

1. **Get your dispatch** from the Dispatch Officer
2. **Verify** the products and quantities match what you received
3. **Count your crates**

### During Sales

4. Keep track of what you sell
5. Handle products carefully (damaged products are your responsibility)

### Returning

6. **Return to the bakery** with unsold products
7. **Report to Dispatch Officer** with:
   - Unsold products (count them)
   - Crates (all of them)
   - Any issues or damaged goods
8. The Dispatch Officer will process your return

### After Return

9. **Check your dispatch** in the system
10. Verify the numbers match your sales
11. See your commission earned

---

## ⚠️ Important Rules

1. **You are responsible for products** given to you
2. **Return all crates** - missing crates may be deducted
3. **Report issues immediately** - damaged goods, theft, etc.
4. **Be honest about returns** - the numbers must match
5. **Commission is paid after returns are processed**

## ❓ Common Questions

### Q: Why can't I see my dispatch?
- The Dispatch Officer may not have created it yet
- Check with the Dispatch Officer
- Make sure you're logged into the correct account

### Q: Why is my commission different than expected?
- Commission is based on what you **sold** (dispatched minus returned)
- Check the product prices used
- Check your commission rate with Admin

### Q: What if I disagree with the return numbers?
- Speak to the Dispatch Officer immediately
- Returns should be processed while you're present
- Verify counts before the dispatch officer submits

### Q: Can I see other salespeople's dispatches?
- No, you can only see your own dispatches
- This protects everyone's information

### Q: When do I get paid my commission?
- Commission is calculated daily (per dispatch)
- Payment schedule is set by management
- Check with your supervisor

### Q: What if I lost products?
- Report to the Dispatch Officer
- It will be recorded as "sold" (lost products = your responsibility)
- May be deducted from your pay
